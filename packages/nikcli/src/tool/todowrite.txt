Create/update a structured todo list for the current session.

Use for multi-step or non-trivial work, explicit user request, or multiple tasks. Skip for single trivial steps or pure Q&A.

States: pending | in_progress | completed | cancelled. Keep at most one in_progress. Mark complete immediately when done; cancel items that become irrelevant.

Break work into specific actionable items. Prefer clarity over granularity theatre.
