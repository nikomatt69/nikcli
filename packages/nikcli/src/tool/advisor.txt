Consult the strategic advisor model for guidance on complex decisions.

Use this tool when you face:
- Ambiguous architectural or design decisions with multiple valid approaches
- Complex multi-step problems where you are unsure which path to take
- Situations where you have attempted something and need a second opinion before continuing
- Tasks that require deep reasoning about trade-offs, risk, or long-term consequences

Do NOT call this tool for:
- Routine coding tasks you can solve directly
- Simple lookups, reads, or writes
- Situations where you already know the correct approach

The advisor does not execute tools or produce user-facing output. It returns a concise plan or recommendation only. You must then act on that guidance yourself.
